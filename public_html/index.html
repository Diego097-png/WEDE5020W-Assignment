Welcome to Readers Den

Readers Den Comic Shop has grown from a small used book stand at the Green Point flea market during the early 1990s into a premier retail destination for pop-culture fans. Owned and managed by brothers Nizar and Mahdi Abrahams, we offer an extensive collection of imported American comic books, Japanese manga, and graphic novels.

Our Store Locations

We operate two physical retail branches in Cape Town to serve our community. Our flagship southern suburbs branch is located at 25 Barnstaple Road in Wynberg, and our northern suburbs branch is situated inside the Tyger Valley Centre.
