Our History and Growth

Readers Den Comic Shop began its journey as a modest used book stand at the Green Point flea market in the early 1990s. Through dedication to the pop-culture community, it expanded into permanent retail storefronts and a robust online presence under the leadership of brothers Nizar and Mahdi Abrahams.

Our Mission

Our mission is to serve as a vital hub for collectors and casual readers alike across South Africa. We strive to make imported US comic books, Japanese manga, and exclusive pop-culture merchandise accessible to everyone who shares a passion for sequential art.

Meet the Founders

Owned and managed by Nizar and Mahdi Abrahams, Readers Den reflects a lifelong passion for comic books and collectibles, building a welcoming environment for fans of all ages.
